## Procedure 1.1: Register Your Business Domain on Hostinger

1. **Open your web browser** and go to the Hostinger domain search page:  
   `https://www.hostinger.com/domains`

2. **Enter a domain name** in the search box (e.g., `supplychainai.com`) and click the **SEARCH** button.

3. **Review the list of available TLDs**.  
   - If you want a .com, click **SELECT** next to the .com option.  
   - If you prefer .ai, click **SELECT** next to the .ai option.

4. **Add the domain to your cart** by clicking the **ADD TO CART** button.  
   *Check‑in:* Do you see a confirmation banner that says “Domain successfully added to cart”? If not, refresh the page and repeat step 4.

5. **Proceed to checkout** by clicking the **PROCEED TO CHECKOUT** button in the cart sidebar.

6. **Create a Hostinger account** (if you don’t have one).  
   - Click **SIGN UP**.  
   - In the pop‑up, enter your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Password** (use a strong password, e.g., `S@f3C0nn3ct!`).  
   - Click the **CREATE ACCOUNT** button.

7. **Verify your email**.  
   - Open your inbox, find the email from Hostinger, click the **VERIFY EMAIL** link.  
   *Check‑in:* Do you see a “Verification Successful” message? If not, check your spam folder.

8. **Return to the checkout page**. The domain should still be in your cart. Click **CONTINUE TO PAYMENT**.

9. **Select a payment plan**.  
   - For a single domain, choose the **1‑Year Plan** for $12.99 (Hostinger’s base price).  
   - Click **SELECT** next to the 1‑Year Plan.

10. **Enter billing details**.  
    - **Card Number**: `4111 1111 1111 1111` (use a test card if you’re testing).  
    - **Expiration Date**: `12/2026`.  
    - **CVC**: `123`.  
    - **Billing ZIP**: `90210`.  
    - Click **CONTINUE**.

11. **Confirm purchase**.  
    - Review the order summary: Domain + 1‑year plan + tax.  
    - Click the **PLACE ORDER** button.

12. **Wait for confirmation**.  
    - You should see a **Thank You** page with the text: “Your domain has been registered successfully.”  
    *Check‑in:* Do you see the “Domain has been registered” confirmation? If not, contact Hostinger support at `https://www.hostinger.com/contact`.

13. **Log into your Hostinger Control Panel**.  
    - Go to `https://www.hostinger.com/cpanel` and sign in with your credentials.

14. **Navigate to the Domain Manager**.  
    - In the left sidebar, click **DOMAINS** → **Domain Manager**.

15. **Verify your domain status**.  
    - Your newly registered domain (`supplychainai.com`) should appear with a status of **ACTIVE**.  
    *Check‑in:* Do you see the domain listed as ACTIVE? If not, click **RELOAD**.

16. **Configure DNS settings** (optional but recommended).  
    - Click **EDIT DNS** next to your domain.  
    - Add an `A` record pointing to your server IP (`123.45.67.89`).  
    - Add a `CNAME` record for `www` pointing to `@`.  
    - Click **SAVE**.

17. **Set up WHOIS privacy protection** (optional).  
    - In Domain Manager, click **WHOIS Privacy** → **ENABLE**.  
    - Confirm by clicking **ENABLE PROTECTION**.  
    *Check‑in:* Do you see “Privacy Enabled” badge? If not, ensure you’re in the correct domain row.

18. **Create an email address** (if needed).  
    - In Domain Manager, click **Email** → **CREATE EMAIL**.  
    - Enter **Username**: `info` and click **CREATE**.  
    - Set a password for the mailbox.

19. **Test domain resolution**.  
    - Open a new browser tab, type `https://supplychainai.com` and press Enter.  
    - You should see a placeholder page or a “Page Not Found” error (since you haven’t set up hosting yet).  
    *Check‑in:* Does the domain resolve? If not, ensure DNS propagation by checking `https://dnschecker.org`.

20. **Set up domain forwarding** (if you want to redirect to another site).  
    - In Domain Manager, click **FORWARDING** → **ADD FORWARD**.  
    - Enter the destination URL (`https://www.example.com`) and choose **Permanent (301)**.  
    - Click **SAVE**.

21. **Document your domain details**.  
    - Open Notion (free tier: $0/month, 1,000 blocks).  
    - Create a new page called **Domain Registration Log**.  
    - Add a table with columns: **Domain**, **Registration Date**, **Expiration**, **Status**, **Privacy**.  
    - Fill in the row for `supplychainai.com`.

22. **Backup your DNS records**.  
    - In Domain Manager, click **EXPORT DNS**.  
    - Save the CSV file to your local drive.

23. **Set up a monitoring alert** using Make.com (free plan: $0/month, 100 operations/month).  
    - Create a new scenario: **When domain status changes** → **Send email via Gmail**.  
    - Configure the trigger to watch the DNS status of `supplychainai.com`.  
    - Test by toggling the status to INACTIVE and confirm you receive an email.

24. **Check for renewal reminders**.  
    - Hostinger sends an email 30 days before renewal.  
    - In your email client, set a calendar event for the renewal date to ensure you don’t miss it.

25. **Close the procedure**.  
    - You have successfully registered, verified, and configured your business domain on Hostinger.  
    - Keep the documentation in Notion updated for future reference.

## Procedure 2.1: Connect ChatGPT API and Store Keys in Notion

1. **Open a web browser** and navigate to the OpenAI API portal:  
   `https://platform.openai.com/api-keys`.  
   *Expected result:* You land on the “API keys” dashboard with a “Create new secret key” button.

2. **Click the button** **Create new secret key** (top‑right).  
   *Expected result:* A modal pops up titled “Create a new API key”.

3. **Name the key** “SupplyChainOptimizer‑ChatGPT” in the **Key name** field.  
   *Expected result:* The field is filled and the key is ready to generate.

4. **Click** **Create secret key** (bottom of the modal).  
   *Expected result:* Your new key appears in the list and a copy icon appears next to it.

5. **Copy the key** by clicking the **Copy** icon.  
   *Expected result:* The key is in your clipboard and a tooltip “Copied!” appears.

6. **Open a new tab** and go to Notion: `https://www.notion.so`.  
   *Expected result:* You see the Notion sign‑in screen.

7. **Sign in** with your email or Google account.  
   *Expected result:* You are taken to your workspace home page.

8. **Create a new database** by clicking **+ New page** (top‑left) → **Table – Full page**.  
   *Expected result:* A blank table opens titled “Untitled”.

9. **Rename the page** to “API Keys – Supply Chain” by clicking the title and typing the new name.  
   *Expected result:* The page title updates.

10. **Add columns** to the table:  
    - Click **+ Add a property** → **Name** → **Title** (keep default).  
    - **+ Add a property** → **Key** → **Text** (choose “Text” type).  
    - **+ Add a property** → **Service** → **Select** (add option “ChatGPT”).  
    - **+ Add a property** → **Expires** → **Date** (leave blank for now).  
    *Expected result:* Four columns appear with the specified types.

11. **Insert your key**:  
    - Click the first empty row under **Title** → type “ChatGPT API Key”.  
    - Under **Key**, paste the key you copied earlier.  
    - Under **Service**, select “ChatGPT”.  
    *Expected result:* The row now contains your key and metadata.

12. **Do you see the row with “ChatGPT API Key” and the key value?**  
    If not, double‑check that you pasted the key in the **Key** column and that the row is saved.

13. **Secure the key**:  
    - Click the three dots (**⋮**) in the upper right of the table → **Table settings**.  
    - Turn on **Hide property** for the **Key** column.  
    *Expected result:* The key column is now hidden; only the title and service are visible.

14. **Create a second database** for future keys (e.g., “Azure Cognitive Services”).  
    Repeat steps 8–13 with the appropriate name and service.

15. **Integrate Notion with Make.com** to automate key rotation:  
    - Open `https://www.make.com` and log in.  
    - Click **Create a scenario** → **Add a trigger** → search and select [**Notion**](https://notion.so/).  
    - Choose **Create a database item** trigger and connect your Notion account.  
    - Map the **Title** field to the **Title** column and **Key** to the **Key** column.  
    *Expected result:* The scenario is ready to fire when a new row is added.

16. **Add an action** in the same scenario:  
    - Click **Add an action** → search **HTTP** → **Make a request**.  
    - Set **Method** to `PUT`, **URL** to `https://api.openai.com/v1/models/gpt-4`, **Headers**:  
      `Authorization: Bearer {{Notion.Key}}` and `Content-Type: application/json`.  
    - **Body**: `{ "name": "SupplyChainOptimizer" }`.  
    *Expected result:* The request will validate the key against OpenAI.

17. **Test the scenario**:  
    - Click **Run once** → follow prompts to authorize Notion and OpenAI.  
    - Observe the **HTTP response** panel.  
    *Expected result:* Status `200 OK` and JSON response containing the model name.

18. **Do you see a 200 OK response?**  
    If not, check the **Authorization** header; if the key is wrong, you’ll see `401 Unauthorized`.

19. **If you see `401 Unauthorized`, this means the key is invalid or expired.**  
    Fix it by:  
    - Regenerating a new key in the OpenAI portal.  
    - Updating the key in the Notion table.  
    - Re‑running the Make scenario.

20. **Schedule the scenario**:  
    - Click **Add schedule** → set to run every 24 hours.  
    *Expected result:* The scenario will auto‑run daily to confirm key validity.

21. **Create a Notion reminder** for key rotation:  
    - In the “API Keys – Supply Chain” database, add a new row under **Expires** with a date 90 days from today.  
    - In Notion, set a **Reminder** on that date to alert you to generate a new key.

22. **Document the process**:  
    - Create a new Notion page titled “ChatGPT API Key Management”.  
    - Copy the steps 1–21 into the page as a checklist.  
    - Add a note: “Keep this page private; set workspace permissions to owner only.”

23. **Verify permissions**:  
    - Click **Share** → **Invite** → add your email with **Can edit**.  
    - Remove any other collaborators.  
    *Expected result:* Only you have edit rights.

24. **Backup the database**:  
    - In Notion, click **⋮** → **Export** → choose **Markdown & CSV** → click **Export**.  
    - Save the ZIP to a secure drive (e.g., Google Drive, one‑time password protected).  
    *Expected result:* A local copy of the key database exists.

25. **Final check**:  
    - Do you see the “API Keys – Supply Chain” page with the hidden key column and a scheduled Make scenario?  
    - If any step is missing, revisit the corresponding number.  
    - Your ChatGPT API key is now securely stored, automatically validated, and ready

# APPENDIX C: THE REVENUE CALCULATOR  

Below you will find the exact operating system for projecting, tracking, and validating revenue for your AI‑powered supply‑chain optimization service. Follow each step precisely; any deviation will distort the financial model and compromise decision‑making.

## 1. Revenue Projection Table  
*You will build this table in a new Notion database titled “Revenue Forecast.” The database must contain the following columns with the exact data types and formulas.*

| Column | Type | Formula / Notes |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| **Month** | Title | Manual entry (1, 3, 6, 12) |
| **Revenue** | Number | `Clients * Avg. Contract Value` |
| **Clients** | Number | Manual entry (see Pricing Tiers section) |
| **Expenses** | Number | Sum of all costs for that month (see Cost Breakdown table) |
| **Profit** | Formula | `Revenue - Expenses` |

### Step‑by‑Step Creation

1. **Open Notion** → Click “+ New Page” → Name it “Revenue Forecast.”  
2. Click “Table – Full Page.”  
3. Click “+ Add a property” → **Month** → **Title**.  
4. Add **Revenue** → **Number**.  
5. Add **Clients** → **Number**.  
6. Add **Expenses** → **Number**.  
7. Add **Profit** → **Formula** → `prop("Revenue") - prop("Expenses")`.  
8. Click “+ New” → Fill in Month = 1, Clients = 5, Avg. Contract Value = $15,000 → Revenue = 5 × $15,000 = $75,000.  
9. Repeat for Months 3, 6, and 12.

**Do you see the Profit column displaying the correct calculation?**  
If not, double‑check that the formula uses the exact property names: `prop("Revenue") - prop("Expenses")`.

## 3. Break‑Even Analysis  
*Build a simple spreadsheet in Google Sheets (free tier, 15 GB storage). Use the exact formulas below.*

| Month | Cumulative Revenue | Cumulative Expenses | Cumulative Profit |
|-------|--------------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | =SUMIF(Revenue Forecast!B:B, "<=1", Revenue Forecast!B:B) | =SUMIF(Revenue Forecast!B:B, "<=1", Revenue Forecast!C:C) | =C2-D2 |
| 3 | =SUMIF(Revenue Forecast!B:B, "<=3", Revenue Forecast!B:B) | =SUMIF(Revenue Forecast!B:B, "<=3", Revenue Forecast!C:C) | =C3-D3 |
| 6 | =SUMIF(Revenue Forecast!B:B, "<=6", Revenue Forecast!B:B) | =SUMIF(Revenue Forecast!B:B, "<=6", Revenue Forecast!C:C) | =C4-D4 |
| 12 | =SUMIF(Revenue Forecast!B:B, "<=12", Revenue Forecast!B:B) | =SUMIF(Revenue Forecast!B:B, "<=12", Revenue Forecast!C:C) | =C5-D5 |

### Instructions

1. Open Google Sheets → “Blank” → Name the sheet “Break‑Even.”  
2. In cell A1 type **Month**; in B1 **Cumulative Revenue**; in C1 **Cumulative Expenses**; in D1 **Cumulative Profit**.  
3. Copy the formulas from the table into cells B2, C2, D2, B3, C3, D3, etc.  
4. Replace `Revenue Forecast!B:B` with the actual Notion‑to‑Sheets integration query if you have set one up; otherwise, manually copy the values from the Notion table into the sheet.
