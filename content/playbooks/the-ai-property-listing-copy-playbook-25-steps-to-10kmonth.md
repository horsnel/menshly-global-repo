## Procedure 1.2: SET UP EMAIL FORWARDING IN ZOHO MAIL

1. **Open a web browser** and navigate to the Zoho Mail sign‑in page:  
   `https://www.zoho.com/mail/`.  
2. **Click the blue button** labeled **“Sign in”** in the top‑right corner.  
3. **Enter your Zoho Mail credentials** (email address and password) in the fields marked **“Username”** and **“Password”**, then click **“Sign in”**.  
4. Once logged in, **click the gear icon** (⚙️) in the upper‑right corner and select **“Settings”** from the dropdown.  
   *Do you see the Settings page with a vertical menu on the left? If not, refresh the page or clear the cache and try again.*

5. In the left‑hand menu, **click “Email Forwarding & POP/IMAP”** under the “Mail Accounts” section.  
6. On the right pane, **click the green button** labeled **“Add Domain”** if you have not yet verified your domain.  
7. **Enter your domain name** (e.g., `mypropertylistings.com`) in the text field and click **“Verify”**.  
8. Zoho will display a **verification code**. **Copy** the code, go to your domain registrar, and paste it into the TXT record.  
   *Do you see the “Verification Successful” banner? If not, double‑check the TXT record and allow up to 48 hours for propagation.*

9. Return to Zoho Mail and **click the “Verify” button** again to confirm the TXT record.  
10. Once verified, **click “Enable Forwarding”**.  
11. In the **“Forward to email address”** field, **enter the destination email** (e.g., `sales@mypropertylistings.com`).  
12. **Select the forwarding options**:  
    - Tick **“Keep a copy of forwarded email in Zoho Mail”** if you want to archive.  
    - Tick **“Delete messages after forwarding”** if you only want to forward.  
13. **Click the blue button** labeled **“Save”**.  
    *Do you see the “Forwarding rules added” notification? If you see “Error: Invalid email address”, confirm the destination address is correct and try again.*

14. **Enable POP/IMAP**: In the same settings section, **check the box** next to **“Enable POP for all mail”** and **“Enable IMAP”**.  
15. Click **“Save”** again to apply POP/IMAP changes.  
    *Do you see the “POP/IMAP settings updated” message? If you see “Error: POP/IMAP already enabled”, the settings are already active; proceed to step 16.*

16. To automate forwarding to a cloud storage, **log into Make.com** at `https://www.make.com/` (free tier: 500 operations/month).  
17. Click **“Create a new scenario”**. In the search bar, type **“Zoho Mail”** and choose the **“Watch New Email”** trigger.  
18. **Connect your Zoho Mail account** by clicking **“Add”**, entering your Zoho credentials, and granting the required permissions.  
19. Add an action: search for **“Google Drive”** (or **“Dropbox”**) and select **“Upload a file”**.  
20. Map the **“Attachment”** field from the Zoho trigger to the **“File”** field in the Google Drive action.  
21. In the Google Drive action, set the **Folder Path** to `/Forwarded_Emails/`.  
22. Click **“Save”** and **activate** the scenario.  
    *Do you see the green “Active” status? If you see “Error: Unauthorized”, re‑authenticate both Zoho and Google accounts.*

23. **Test the forwarding**: send a test email to your Zoho inbox.  
24. Verify that the email appears in your Gmail or destination inbox and that the attachment is uploaded to the designated Google Drive folder.  
25. **Document the setup**: open Notion (free tier: 5 GB), create a page titled “Zoho Email Forwarding Setup”, and paste the screenshots of the Settings page, Make.com scenario, and confirmation email.

import openai, csv

openai.api_key = API_KEY

def generate_copy(address, city, beds, baths, sqft, price, notes):
    prompt = f"""Write a compelling real‑estate listing description for the following property.

Address: {address}
City: {city}
Bedrooms: {beds}
Bathrooms: {baths}
Square footage: {sqft}
Price: ${price:,}
Highlights: {notes}

The description should be 150‑200 words, highlight unique selling points, and include a call‑to‑action encouraging prospective buyers to schedule a showing.  
Return only the description, no extra commentary.  
"""
    response = openai.ChatCompletion.create(

    )
    return response["choices"][0]["message"]["content"].strip()
