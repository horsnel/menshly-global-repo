## Pricing: What to Charge and How to Defend It

### Starter – $500/Month  
You’re just flipping gigs, not launching a full service.  
- 5 projects a month, each capped at 20 h.  
- Use [Make.com](https://www.make.com/en/register?pc=menshly) to auto‑schedule 80 % of the workflow.  
- Deliver via Loom video walkthroughs.  
- Basic analytics from Google Analytics and a Klaviyo email recap.  

You’re not selling a boutique experience; you’re pitching speed and automation. Tell clients the price covers the $0.99/month [Replit](https://replit.com/refer/egwuokwor) credits for the AI scripts they’ll run and the $10/month [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) Pro license to polish visuals. If they want more hours, you raise the hourly rate to $70.

### Growth – $1,500/Month  
Now you’re handling the real work that deserves a premium.  
- 12 projects, 40 h total, split between content & design.  
- Use Zapier to link every tool, from Apollo.io for lead capture to Buffer for social posting.  
- Include a 30‑minute strategy call via Calendly each month.  
- Deliver assets in an organized [Notion](https://notion.so/) workspace, ready for handoff.  
- Add a 10% discount on the next month if you close a retainer sign‑up.  

Clients see the value: the $1,500 covers 4 hours of consultancy + the $49/month Hostinger plan that hosts their landing page, plus the $25/month [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/) voice‑synthesis for podcasts or explainer videos. If they need extra hours, you’ll bill $100/h.

### Enterprise – $5,000/Month  
The premium tier for clients who want a full‑blown AI studio.  
- Unlimited projects, but capped at 80 h of pure labor.  
- 24/7 support via a dedicated Slack channel.  
- Monthly performance audit using [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) and a custom Midjourney image library.  
- Integration of [Vapi](https://vapi.ai/) voice agents into their customer support flow.  
- Quarterly live workshop on scaling AI tools.  

You defend the price with a portfolio: a case study showing a 200 % ROI for a client using your AI‑driven funnel. Mention the $500/month for an active campaign license and the $200/month for a custom AI model trained on their data. If they ask for more hours, set a threshold: over 80 h, bill $75/h.

**[accent-box]**  
**Pricing Hack**  
Bundle a free 30‑minute audit for leads who sign up for a 3‑month trial. Then upsell them to Growth at $1,500. The audit costs only $50 in tools (Zapier + Notion templates), but it unlocks a 20 % conversion bump.  

Keep the numbers visible. Clients love seeing the cost of every tool: Replit ($9.99/month), Canva Pro ($12.99/month), Hostinger ($3.99/month), ElevenLabs ($99/month). When you build the price sheet in Notion, pin it to the top of the client dashboard so they can’t miss it.  
---

This tiered structure lets you scale without losing control. Stay transparent, keep the tool stack lean, and watch the cash flow roll in.
