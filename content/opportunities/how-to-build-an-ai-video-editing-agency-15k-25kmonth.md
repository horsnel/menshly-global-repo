## The Free Stack: Starting With Zero Dollars  

[**Canva — $0**](https://www.canva.com/) — Drag‑and‑drop design for thumbnails, intros, and captions.  
[**Notion — $0**](https://notion.so/) — All‑in‑one workspace for scripts, project plans, and meeting notes.  
[**Replit — $0**](https://replit.com/refer/egwuokwor) — Run Python scripts to trim, color‑grade, or add basic effects online.  
**ChatGPT — $0** — Generate cuts, captions, and creative copy in seconds.  
**Loom — $0** — Record quick walkthroughs of your edits for client feedback.  
**Calendly — $0** — Schedule discovery calls without the back‑and‑forth email loop.  
**Buffer — $0** — Push client‑approved videos to TikTok, IG, and YouTube on autopilot.

The free tiers feel great, but they’re not built for scale.  
Canva’s free plan locks you into 5GB of storage and hides the brand‑able templates that professional clients expect.  
Notion caps you at 5,000 blocks; once you have a dozen projects, you hit that limit fast.  
Replit’s free tier throttles CPU time and blocks paid libraries, which means you can’t run heavy‑weight video codecs.  
ChatGPT’s free version limits you to 3–5k tokens per month, so you’ll hit a wall when you need to script longer montages.  
Loom’s free account cuts video length at 5 minutes; you’ll need the Pro plan for longer client demos.  
Calendly’s free plan restricts you to a single calendar; no integrations with your favorite CRMs yet.  
Buffer’s free tier only lets you schedule 10 posts per social account, so your content calendar stalls.  

You can get a lot off the ground, but once you start pulling in clients, you’ll see the gaps.  
Upgrade to Canva Pro ($12.99/mo) for unlimited storage and brand kits.  
Notion Plus ($4/mo) lifts the block limit and adds version history.  
Replit Hacker ($7/mo) gives you unlimited CPU and private repos.  
ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) unlocks the 3‑month memory, higher token limits, and priority API access.  
Loom Pro ($10/mo) for unlimited recording length.  
Calendly Pro ($8/mo) adds meeting types, custom branding, and team scheduling.  
Buffer Premium ($12/mo per account) expands your posting window to 100 per month.  

When you hit the limits, the upgrade is the only way to keep the workflow smooth and avoid the annoying “upgrade now” pop‑ups that break client trust.

## The Workflow: Step-by-Step With Every Shortcut

Book a 15‑minute discovery call with Calendly; the link auto‑fills the Zoom invite and puts the client on your Google Calendar. > “Create a question list for a 30‑second product promo: hook, pain point, solution, CTA, brand colors, preferred music style.”

## Getting Clients: The Real Playbook  

### Method 1: LinkedIn Prospecting with PhantomBuster & Apollo.io (Conversion Rate: 30%)  
Build a list in seconds with PhantomBuster. Run the “People Search” script on your target industry. 500 free operations every day give you 15,000 contacts in a month. Export to CSV, then import into Apollo.io. Use Apollo’s free tier – 500 leads/month – to send personalized emails. Write a 4‑sentence pitch: “Hey [Name], I see you’re crushing growth at [Company]. I just launched an AI‑powered video editing service that cut your post‑production time by 70%. Want to see a quick demo?” Follow up with a second email after 3 days. Track opens in Apollo, then move the hot leads to ActiveCampaign for nurture. The combo cuts your outreach time to under 2 hours per week and gives a 30% reply rate.  

### Method 2: Niche Video Direct Mail (Conversion Rate: 15%)  
Create a viral hook video using Fliki AI ($29/month) and Midjourney for custom thumbnails. Upload to YouTube Shorts, tag with niche keywords. Use [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) to find the top 10 long‑tail keywords that drive 5‑7,000 searches/month for “video editing for fitness coaches.” Optimize the title and description. Run a paid retargeting ad on Instagram ($5/day) to those who watched ≥50% of the Short. Simultaneously, set up a Canva Pro ($12.99/month) landing page with a free 5‑minute editing demo. Capture email with a simple form; hand that list over to ActiveCampaign for a drip series: “Your free demo”, “Case study: 30‑second reels for 200% engagement”, “Book a 15‑minute strategy call”. The funnel is low‑cost: $12.99 + $29 + $5 = $46.99/month. The 15% conversion is the trimmed‑down open rate of the email series to booked calls.  

### Method 3: Content Funnel with YouTube Shorts + Buffer + Loom (Conversion Rate: 5%)  
Post a 30‑second “before/after” edit every week on YouTube Shorts. Use Buffer’s free plan to schedule 10 posts a day. In the description, link to a Loom video (free 2,000 minutes/month). The Loom video is a one‑minute pitch: “Hi, I’m Alex from AI‑Video Solutions. The funnel is 100% organic; the 5% conversion comes from the number of viewers that book a call after watching the Loom.
