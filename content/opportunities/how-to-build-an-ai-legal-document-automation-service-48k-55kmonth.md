## Pricing: What to Charge and How to Defend It

### Starter – $499/month  
You’re just getting the ball rolling. Charge this flat fee for a single‑page contract template—say, a nondisclosure agreement (NDA). What you deliver:  
- Custom‑tailored PDF generated via [**Make.com**](https://www.make.com/en/register?pc=menshly) and [**Replit**](https://replit.com/refer/egwuokwor).  
- One‑off revisions (up to 3).  
- Access to a private [**Notion**](https://notion.so/) workspace where you keep the template library.  

Why $499? It covers your time building the template, the Make.com plan $49/month, and the Replit Cloud IDE credits $20/month. Clients get a ready‑to‑use legal doc that saves them a lawyer bill, so the price feels like a bargain compared to $3,000 for a traditional attorney.

### Growth – $1,999/month  
Now you’re doing multiple documents and onboarding clients. Include:  
- Unlimited single‑page contracts (NDAs, LOIs, leases).  
- 5 revisions per month.  
- Automated email notifications via **Klaviyo** (starts at $20/month).  
- Monthly audit of your document library in [**Semrush**](https://www.semrush.com/) for compliance keyword checks.  
- A 30‑minute strategy call each quarter.  

You’re adding a **Make.com** premium plan ($99) to handle more triggers, and a **Klaviyo** plan to segment clients. The price reflects the extra value: faster turnaround, automated marketing, and guaranteed compliance. Clients feel they’re buying a full service, not just a document.

### Enterprise – $5,999/month  
For law firms or large SMBs who need an entire contract ecosystem:  
- Unlimited documents plus custom templates for every niche (employment, product liability, etc.).  
- Unlimited revisions.  
- Dedicated **ActiveCampaign** pipeline for client management.  
- Quarterly compliance workshops (video via [**Fliki AI**](https://fliki.ai?referral=noah-wilson-w84be4) for 2-hour sessions).  
- 1‑on‑1 account manager call each month.  
- API access for your clients to pull templates directly into their own systems.  

You’re covering the cost of the **ActiveCampaign** plan ($69/month), **Fliki AI** video credits ($15/month), and an extra $1,000 for legal counsel time per month to ensure every template meets current statutes. Clients pay because the ROI is clear: they get a fully compliant, scalable contract system that reduces attorney hours by 70%.

### Pricing Trick HACK – Accent‑Box  
```accent-box  
Bundle your Starter tier with a 3‑month free trial of [**Canva Pro**](https://www.canva.com/) (for branding). Offer it at $0 for the first 90 days. This upsells clients to the Growth tier by the time their trial ends, pushing them past $1,999/month. The perceived value spikes, and conversion jumps 25%.  
```  

**Defend Your Rates**  
Every dollar is backed by a tangible tool cost and a measurable benefit. When a client asks, point to the Make.com automation that cuts document prep from 8 hours to 30 minutes, or the Klaviyo segmentation that increases renewal rates by 15%. If they still balk, offer a “pay‑as‑you‑go” version of the Starter tier at $399/month—still profitable because your tool spend is fixed. Keep the conversation focused on value, not price.
