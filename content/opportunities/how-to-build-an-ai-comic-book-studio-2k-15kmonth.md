Drop the “–no background” flag to let the AI fill in the backdrop, saving you a whole extra image. If you run 100 panels a month, you’ll only spend 50 credits – $10 of your $30 pro budget. The trick is to batch your prompts: put 5 scenes in one prompt, separated by semicolons, and let Midjourney do the grouping. The AI learns the style and spits out coherent page breaks. It cuts your image cost by 40% and keeps the line quality high.

## What Nobody Warns You About

**1. The “Free” Images Are Not Free**  
Midjourney’s $30/month pro tier gives you 2000 image credits, but that’s only the production cost. Every time you sell a comic, you need a separate commercial license that costs $1 per image. If you print 10 editions a month, that’s 100 images, so $100 a month just for rights. That’s 35% of your revenue. Forget about a $0 license; it’s a monthly line item you need to budget for.  

**2. Prompt‑to‑Prompt Hell**  
AI can’t always stay on brand. Midjourney flags some prompts that violate its policy, and you lose a credit for each rejection. Replit hosts a quick Python script to audit your prompt list and re‑generate images in bulk, costing about $30/month. Add that to your stack, and you’re paying for “clean prompts.” A single mis‑step can cost you a credit and a day of work, so treat every prompt like a paid ad.  

**3. Design & Distribution Fees Stack**  
Once you’ve got the art, you need layout, copy, and a storefront. Notion (free tier) is great for scripts, but Canva Pro at $12.99/month gives you templates and export options. A Shopify store costs $29/month plus 2% transaction fees; every sale carries a hidden fee. Add to that a Buffer Pro account ($15/month) for scheduling posts, and you’re already spending 5–6% of gross revenue on tooling.  

**4. Customer Acquisition Becomes a Mirage**  
You might think “just drop a PDF and call it a day.” In reality, you need an email list, a funnel, and paid traffic. Klaviyo’s starter plan starts at $20/month, but to acquire 1,000 buyers you’ll likely need to spend $500 on Facebook ads, plus $30 on Apollo.io for B2B outreach. That’s a 15–20% marketing spend that can wipe out any “$2K/month” headline. Pay close attention to CAC before you scale.
