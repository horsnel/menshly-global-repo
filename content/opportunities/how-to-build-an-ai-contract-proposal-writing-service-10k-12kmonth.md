## The Free Stack: Starting With Zero Dollars

[**Notion — $0**](https://notion.so/) — All-in-one workspace for projects, docs, and templates.  
[**Grammarly — $0**](https://grammarly.com/) — Clean up typos and style in every line.  
**Zapier — $0** — 5 integrations, 100 tasks/month.  
[**Canva — $0**](https://www.canva.com/) — Basic design kits for headers and covers.  
**ChatGPT — $0** — 3‑month free trial of ChatGPT‑4.  
**Loom — $0** — Record quick video proposals, shareable link.  
[**Replit — $0**](https://replit.com/refer/egwuokwor) — Run Python scripts to auto‑populate template slots.

You’ll be on track with the tools that already have generous free tiers. Each one does a lot, and you can chain them together with Zapier. For example, pull a client’s data from a Google Sheet, feed it into a ChatGPT prompt, then push the output to a Notion page, and finally export as a PDF. The free tiers let you test this workflow, keep costs down, and show proof of concept to the first clients.

But the free stack is a sandbox, not a launchpad.  
- **Zapier’s 100‑task cap** means you’ll hit the ceiling fast if you have 10 proposals a week.  
- **Notion’s limited file storage** forces you to compress documents or split them across pages.  
- **ChatGPT’s rate limits** often slow you down during crunch time.  
- **Canva’s free templates** are generic; brands want eye‑catching, custom branding.  
- **Loom’s free plan** restricts video length to 25 minutes and adds a watermark.  
- **Replit’s free tier** has a 500 MB disk quota and no persistent storage.  
- **Grammarly’s free version** doesn’t catch passive‑voice or tone issues.

It’s fine to keep rolling with this stack while you’re still experimenting. The moment you land that 12‑week project that makes you $12 k, you’ll feel the pinch. When you’re hitting the Zapier limit or delivering polished, brand‑specific proposals, it’s time to upgrade. Pay for Zapier’s paid plans, get Notion’s Enterprise plan for unlimited blocks and advanced permissions, and switch ChatGPT‑4 to a paid subscription for higher usage. With a $19/month Zapier plan, you get 3,000 tasks and 20 integrations, which is a sweet spot for a solo founder. Adding Canva Pro gives you brand kits and bulk exports. Replit’s Hacker plan lifts the disk quota to 5 GB, so you can keep your own template libraries.

In short, the free stack proves the concept, the paid stack scales the revenue, and the real magic happens when you stop trading minutes for money and start charging what your expertise is worth.

[accent-box] **HACK:** Hook Zapier’s “Create PDF” action to Notion. It turns a plain text block into a downloadable PDF in under 30 seconds, eliminating a whole step of manual conversion. [/accent-box]
