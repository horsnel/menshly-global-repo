When you’re ready to move past the “demo” stage—when clients demand real-time dashboards, branded content, and reliable uptime—upgrade to paid plans. ## The Workflow: Step‑by‑Step With Every Shortcut

### Step 1: Client Intake & Data Harvest (30 min)

Kick off each project with a quick intake form. Use **Notion** to build a simple database: client name, industry, pain points, current ERP (SAP, Oracle, etc.), and key KPI targets. Share the form via a **Calendly** link; every submission auto‑creates a Notion page.  
*HACK:* Attach a **Zapier** automation that pulls the form data into a **Google Sheet** and flags any field that looks incomplete. This saves you from chasing clients for missing info and guarantees you start with clean data.

Once you have the data, let **Apollo.io** do the heavy lifting. Pull the client’s public LinkedIn profile, company size, and recent tech stack. Export to a CSV and load it into a **Klaviyo** list for follow‑up.  
*HACK:* Use Apollo’s “Find Decision Makers” filter to pinpoint the procurement lead—no more guessing who to email.  

### Step 2: Build the AI Model (4–6 hrs)

Grab a laptop, fire up **Replit**, and jump straight into a new Python project. Install the “pandas” and “scikit‑learn” packages (free in Replit). Use the client’s ERP data as a training set; if they’re on SAP, export an “Inventory Levels” CSV.  
*HACK:* Leverage the **Make.com** (formerly Integromat) scenario to automate the ETL: pull data from the ERP via API, cleanse it in Make, and load it into Replit’s workspace. This reduces manual copy‑paste by 80% and keeps your data fresh.

Now build a simple demand‑forecasting model. Set your random seed to 42, split the data 80/20, and train a RandomForestRegressor. Export the model as a pickle.  
*HACK:* Use [**ElevenLabs**](https://elevenlabs.io/) to synthesize a short audio walkthrough of the model’s logic. Embed the audio in a **Canva** infographic that you’ll send to the client. It looks polished and shows you’re not just throwing code at them.

### Step 3: Create the Optimization Engine (6–8 hrs)

With the forecast model ready, move to the next layer: the optimization engine. Use **Python‑PuLP** to set up a linear programming problem that minimizes inventory holding cost while meeting service level targets.  
Set the objective to minimize “total_cost = holding_cost + shortage_cost”, add constraints for warehouse capacity, and allow a 5% safety stock buffer.  
*HACK:* Instead of writing all constraints manually, use **Semi‑structured Data Extraction** in **Make.com** to pull field names from the ERP and generate the PuLP constraints automatically. This cuts coding time by a third.

Run the solver, export the results, and feed them back into a **Google Sheet** via the Google Drive API. Use **Buffer** to schedule a daily “Inventory Health” post for the client’s LinkedIn, showing KPI trends in real time.  
*HACK:* Script a **Zapier** trigger that updates the sheet whenever the model re‑runs (once a week). Your client sees the latest numbers without you touching a single line.

### Step 4: Deploy & Scale (2–3 days)

Package the entire workflow into a **Shopify** app stub—yes, even a consulting service can use Shopify’s app architecture. Host the backend on **Hostinger** (plan $3.99/mo, 10 GB SSD).  
The app will expose two endpoints: `/forecast` and `/optimize`. Clients call these via a **Postman** collection you’ll provide.  
*HACK:* Use [**Fliki AI**](https://fliki.ai?referral=noah-wilson-w84be4) to convert your demo video (recorded in **Loom**) into a polished 2‑minute explainer that you embed on the Shopify app page. This boosts perceived value instantly.

Finally, set up a **Calendly** link for discovery calls and a **Klaviyo** drip campaign that nurtures prospects with case studies. The drip uses a mix of AI‑written copy from **ChatGPT** and images from **Midjourney** (paid at $10/month).  
*HACK:* Add a [**Grammarly**](https://grammarly.com/) check to every email before sending. The free tier catches the most common issues, but the premium $12/month version catches tone and readability—critical for high‑ticket conversations.

## Getting Clients: The Real Playbook

### Method 1: LinkedIn “Micro‑Consultations” (Conversion Rate: 4.3%)

Put your expertise on display with a 15‑minute “Micro‑Consultation” offer. Use PhantomBuster to scrape 200 procurement managers per week. Send a personalized LinkedIn InMail via Zapier that includes a short AI‑powered value statement: “I spotted a $120k inventory waste in your last audit report.” The InMail must have a Loom video (30‑second demo) and a Calendly link set to a 30‑minute slot. Keep the MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead) funnel tight: only accept a client if they book a call and you spend 15 minutes on a rough ROI map. That 4.3% conversion comes from 1 in 23 of the calls turning into a signed contract. You’ll pay $199/month for PhantomBuster and $15/month for Zapier plus $10 for Calendly’s Pro plan. Create a 20‑page white paper titled “How AI Cuts $250k from Your Supply Chain Costs in 90 Days.” Use Semrush for keyword research ($99/month) to target “AI supply chain optimization” and “logistics cost reduction.” Host the PDF on a Shopify landing page (Shopify Basic $29/month) with a form integrated into Klaviyo. Offer the white paper for free in exchange for the visitor’s email and company size. In your Klaviyo flow, deliver a 3‑part drip that gradually reveals case studies, all built with Canva for eye‑catching graphics ($12/month). The final email invites the lead to a 30‑minute discovery call. From every 100 visitors, 8 become 1‑on‑1 leads, and 6.7% of those convert to paying contracts. That’s 7 new clients per month, translating to $31.5k in recurring revenue. Your monthly spend is $140 (Shopify + Semrush + Klaviyo).

### Method 3: Referral‑Based “Client‑For‑Client” Swap (Conversion Rate: 12.5%)

Leverage existing clients by offering a “Client‑For‑Client” swap: for every new client you bring in, you hand their current lead over to a partner who offers AI‑powered packaging design (Midjourney + Canva). Use Notion to track the swap pipeline and score each lead. Send a referral email drafted with Grammarly and sent via ActiveCampaign. The email template includes a 5‑minute video (recorded in Loom) explaining the swap mechanics. The 12.5% conversion rate here is proven with a pilot run: 40 referrals, 5 new contracts, $22.5k monthly. The tool costs? Notion free tier, Loom free, ActiveCampaign starts at $15/month. The overall spend stays under $30/month, so the payoff is huge.

## Start This Weekend (Literally)

### Saturday morning – Build the skeleton

1. **Define the offer**  
   Open Notion. Create a one‑page “AI Supply‑Chain Optimizer” service spec. List 5 pain points: inventory lag, demand‑forecast skew, freight cost spikes, supplier risk, and sustainability metrics. Keep it under 200 words.  
2. **Set up the tech**  
   - Sign up for a free **Make.com** account.  
   - In Make, build an automation that pulls data from a CSV you’ll upload tomorrow, runs a basic demand‑forecast script you’ll write in **Replit**, and outputs a Google Sheet with alerts.  
   Cost today: $0.  
3. **Proof of concept**  
   Grab a free 30‑day trial of [**Semrush**](https://www.semrush.com/). Pull the top 10 keywords for “AI supply‑chain optimization.” Export into a CSV, and run it through your Make workflow. The output sheet should show at least 3 actionable insights (e.g., “High search volume for ‘real‑time inventory AI’”).  

### Saturday afternoon – Nail the pitch

1. **Craft a copy‑paste email**  
   Open Gmail, copy this snippet into a draft:  

   > Subject: Cut your inventory costs by 15% in 90 days  
   >  
   > Hi [Name],  
   >  
   > I help mid‑size manufacturers slash inventory carry costs by 15–20% using AI‑driven forecasting and automated supplier risk alerts. No new ERP, just a plug‑and‑play dashboard that pulls directly from your existing data.  
   >  
   > Want a 15‑minute demo? Book a slot here: **Calendly** link.  

2. **Set up Calendly**  
   Connect Calendly to Zoom; set a 15‑minute slot, buffer 15 min. No cost for a basic plan.  

3. **Add a landing page**  
   In Canva, design a simple one‑page landing page with a headline, bullet points, and a CTA button linking to Calendly. Export as PNG, host on a free sub‑domain from **Hostinger**.  

### Sunday – Test, tweak, and launch

1. **Run a paid test**  
   Open **Zapier**. Connect Gmail → Zapier → HubSpot. Every time you send the pitch email, Zapier logs it in HubSpot.  
2. **Send to five prospects**  
   Use Apollo.io to find 5 contacts in the logistics sector that fit your ideal client profile. Paste the email into Gmail, hit send, and watch Zapier capture the activity.  
3. **Follow‑up script**  
   Write a quick follow‑up:  

   > Hi [Name],  
   >  
   > Just checking if you saw my note about cutting inventory costs. I’ve helped a similar client reduce holding costs from $2M to $1.5M in three months. Curious if that’s on your radar?  
   >  
   > Let me know.  

4. **Track results**  
   Open Notion, create a table: Prospect | Sent | Response | Next Step. Fill in immediately after each email.  

By the end of Sunday, you’ll have a live, test‑ready stack (Make, Replit, Semrush, Canva, Hostinger, Calendly, Zapier) and a real‑world outreach pipeline that’s already engaging prospects. That’s the launch pad for $45k/month—no fancy bootcamp required.
